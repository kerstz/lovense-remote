## 💡 In short

- **One app instead of ten.** Stop installing a different app (and creating a
  different account) for every brand: this app speaks the Bluetooth protocol of
  **803 toy models from 127 brand families**.
- **Several toys at the same time.** Connect up to 5 toys — even from different
  brands — and drive them one by one or all together.
- **The screen adapts to your toy.** A vibrator gets a vibration slider, a
  rotating toy gets a direction button, a heated toy gets a heat switch, a
  stroker gets a speed control… you only see what your toy can do.
- **Private by design.** Everything stays on your phone. No sign-up, no
  tracking, no Google services. Sharing with a partner is optional and locked
  behind a PIN and your approval.

## 🚀 Quick start

1. **Install** the APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/kerstz/lovense-remote/releases/latest)
   (Android 8.0+).
2. **Turn your toy on** — and make sure its official app is closed, since a toy
   only accepts one connection at a time.
3. **Open the app.** It searches for nearby toys on its own; yours appears with
   its brand and model name. Tap it to connect (tap others to add more toys).
4. **Play.** Use the sliders, the XY pad, presets or patterns. **STOP ALL** (also
   available as a quick-settings tile) stops everything instantly.

A few toys need an extra pairing step, and the app tells you when: press the
**power button** on Lelo toys, enter **PIN 6496** on Lioness.

## 🔗 Remote control by link

1. Tap **Share**: you get a *Same Wi-Fi* link and an *Internet* link.
2. Send the link to your partner, and tell them the **6-digit PIN** separately.
3. When they open it, **you approve them** on your phone. Only then can they
   drive your toys, from any browser — no app needed on their side.
4. Stop sharing whenever you want; it also expires on its own after 30 minutes.

## 🔒 Security & privacy

Knowing the link is never enough to control your toys:

- **Secret link** — 128-bit random id, new on every share; old links die at once.
- **6-digit PIN** — never in the URL, checked in constant time; **5 wrong codes
  stop the share**.
- **Your approval, per person** — you accept (or kick) each controller
  individually.
- **Hardened web page** — strict CSP, anti-framing, `Origin` check against
  cross-site WebSocket hijacking, small frames, rate limiting, max 3 sockets,
  10 s to authenticate.
- **Minimal network surface** — listens only on 127.0.0.1 and the Wi-Fi IP,
  never on mobile data / VPN interfaces.
- **Pinned relay key** — nobody can impersonate the internet relay.
- **No backups** of app data; deep links are validated and need your OK.

**Limits you should know:** same-Wi-Fi sharing is plain HTTP (use a Wi-Fi you
trust). Internet sharing goes through [localhost.run](https://localhost.run), a
third party that can see the control traffic — the PIN and your approval remain
the gate. If you use a firewall app, allow **network access** for this app or
sharing can't start.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>My toy isn't in the scan.</b></summary>

Make sure it's switched on, not connected to its official app or another phone,
and that Bluetooth and location/nearby-devices permissions are granted. Then
check [the list](docs/SUPPORTED_TOYS.md): if it's not there, open an issue with
the name the toy shows in a BLE scanner app.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What does "experimental" mean? Can it damage my toy?</b></summary>

It means the commands come from community documentation and haven't been tried
on that model by this project. The app only sends the same kind of commands the
official apps send, and only to the channels that model is known to use.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why is it called "Lovense Remote" if it supports other brands?</b></summary>

It started as a Lovense-only remote; multi-brand support came later. It is not
affiliated with Lovense or any other brand.
</details>

## 🛠 For developers

```bash
ANDROID_HOME=~/Android/Sdk ./gradlew :app:assembleDebug   # build
./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest                           # unit tests
```

Kotlin + Jetpack Compose · minSdk 26 · target/compile SDK 35.

<details>
<summary>🧩 Architecture</summary>

- `ble/` — scan and identification (`ToyManager`), one `ToyConnection` per toy
  (GATT with named endpoints, serialized operations, coalesced writes,
  keepalive, stroke generator, battery, reconnection).
- `ble/db/` — the device database (`assets/devices.json`, generated from the
  Buttplug device config by `tools/gen_devices.py`).
- `ble/proto/` — one `ProtocolHandler` per protocol (~128), ported from
  Buttplug: handshakes, encryption, checksums, per-function commands. See
  [`docs/research/other-brands.md`](docs/research/other-brands.md) and
  [`docs/research/lovense-ble-protocol.md`](docs/research/lovense-ble-protocol.md).
- `RemoteEngine` — process-wide core (BLE + server + tunnel + state), so control
  survives the app being closed.
- `remote/` — embedded HTTP + WebSocket server, web control page, internet
  tunnel (`SshTunnel`).

Regenerate the supported-toys list after updating the database:
`python3 tools/gen_supported.py`.
</details>
