One remote for 800+ toys.

Stop installing a different app, and creating a different account, for every brand. This one talks Bluetooth to Lovense, Satisfyer, We-Vibe, Svakom, Lelo, Kiiroo, JoyHub and 120 more. No cloud, no sign-up, free and open source.

    Motor 150% Motor 250%

    This is the app's XY pad: one thumb drives both motors of a two-motor toy.

    The screen adapts to your toy

    You only see what your toy can do. A vibrator gets a slider, a rotating toy gets a direction button, a heated toy gets a switch. Connect up to five toys, from different brands, and drive them one by one or all together.

    Lovense is tested on real hardware. Every other brand is marked experimental: the commands come from the open Buttplug community database and haven't been tried on that model by this project. Tell us if yours works.

    Private by design

    Everything stays on your phone. Sharing with a partner is optional, and knowing the link is never enough to take control.

    • Secret link, new every time. 128-bit random id; old links die at once.
    • 6-digit PIN, never in the URL. Five wrong codes stop the share.
    • You approve each person. Accept or kick controllers one by one.
    • No Google services, no tracking. 100% AOSP: works on GrapheneOS and LineageOS.
